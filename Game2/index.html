THE PUFFY LOOP

  1. 1 Hold X to SUCK an enemy into your mouth
  2. 2 Tap X to SPIT it out — it flies and hurts
  3. 3 Press ↓ to SWALLOW it and copy its power
  4. 4 Tap X to fire the power — it never runs out
  5. 5 Hold ↓ and tap Z to throw the power away
  6. 6 Reach the glowing CAPSULE to clear the stage
